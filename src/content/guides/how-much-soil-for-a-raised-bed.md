**Multiply the bed's inside length × width (in feet) × soil depth (in inches) ÷ 12 to get cubic feet. A 4 × 8 ft bed filled 12 inches deep holds 32 cubic feet: about 1.2 cubic yards, or 16 bags of 2 cu ft.** Measure inside the frame, not outside it, since the boards take up a few inches.

## Fill a little higher than you think

Fresh soil mixes settle as they're watered and as compost breaks down. Many gardeners fill to the top of the frame, expecting it to sink an inch or two over the first season, and top up with compost each spring. If you're measuring the depth you want after settling, order 10 to 20% extra.

## Worked example: three beds

Three 4 × 8 ft beds made from 2×12 boards, filled to 11 in (the boards are 11 1/4 in tall):

1. One bed: 8 × 4 × 11 ÷ 12 = **29.3 cu ft**.
2. Three beds: **88 cu ft** = 3.26 cubic yards.
3. In bags: 20 bags of 1.5 cu ft per bed, 60 in all. In bulk: about 3.5 cubic yards of a raised-bed or garden mix.

At this size bulk is usually cheaper. Ask the supplier what's in their mix, and whether they'll deliver onto a tarp near the beds. Our guide to [bags in a yard](/guides/how-many-bags-of-mulch-in-a-yard/) shows how to compare bag and bulk prices; the same math works for soil.

## What to fill with

A raised bed needs loose, fertile soil that drains well. Common approaches:

- **A bagged or bulk "raised bed mix"** made for the purpose. Easiest, and consistent.
- **Topsoil blended with compost**, often around two-thirds topsoil to one-third compost, adjusted for your soil and plants.
- **Bulk filler in deep beds.** For beds deeper than about 18 in, some gardeners fill the bottom with logs, branches or coarse organic material to cut the soil bill. It decomposes and settles over time.

Garden soil from bags and bulk varies a lot. If your extension service offers soil testing, a test after the first season tells you what to add.

## Topping up each year

Plan on adding 1 to 2 in of compost each spring. For a 4 × 8 bed that's 2.7 to 5.3 cu ft. The [mulch calculator](/mulch-calculator/) handles top-ups: enter the target depth and what's already there, and it counts the difference in bags or yards.
