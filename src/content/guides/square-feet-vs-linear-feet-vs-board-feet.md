**Square feet measure a surface (length × width). Linear feet measure length only, like a run of baseboard or a board laid end to end. Board feet measure the volume of lumber: thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12.** You can only convert between them when you know the missing dimension, such as the width of the boards.

## Square feet

Area of a flat surface. For a rectangle it's length × width, both in feet. A room that isn't a rectangle can be split into rectangles and added up; see our guide to [measuring an L-shaped room](/guides/how-to-measure-an-l-shaped-room/), or use the [square footage calculator](/square-footage-calculator/), which also converts to square yards, square meters and acres.

## Linear feet

Just length, ignoring width. Baseboard, crown molding, gutters, fence and countertop edges are sold or priced this way. For trim, the linear footage of a room is its perimeter minus the doorways. A 12 × 14 ft room has a perimeter of 52 ft; take off a 3 ft door and you need about **49 linear feet** of baseboard, plus a little extra for miter cuts.

## From square feet to linear feet of boards

To cover an area with boards (decking, shiplap, flooring sold by the piece), divide by the width each board covers, in feet:

**Linear feet = square feet ÷ (board width in inches ÷ 12)**

Add your gap to the board width if there is one, and a waste allowance on top. The [deck calculator](/deck-calculator/) handles both.

## Board feet

A board foot is a piece of wood 12 in × 12 in × 1 in: 144 cubic inches. Hardwood lumberyards price by the board foot, and some framing lumber is quoted that way too. The formula uses nominal sizes:

**Board feet = thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12**

So a 2×6 that is 12 ft long is 2 × 6 × 12 ÷ 12 = 12 board feet. If a yard quotes $1.10 per board foot, that board costs $13.20.

## Quick conversions

- **Square feet to square yards:** divide by 9 (carpet is often sold by the square yard).
- **Square inches to square feet:** divide by 144.
- **Cubic feet to cubic yards:** divide by 27 (concrete, gravel, mulch).
- **Linear feet to board feet:** multiply by thickness × width ÷ 12. For a 2×4, each linear foot is 0.667 board feet.

## Which one do you need?

Look at how the material is sold. Flooring and tile come in boxes labeled in square feet. Trim comes in pieces of a set length, so you need linear feet and then round up to whole pieces. Hardwood for a project comes by the board foot, and you should add 20 to 30% for defects and cutting when buying rough stock.
