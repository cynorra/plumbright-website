**Pickets = fence length in inches ÷ (picket width + gap).** For a privacy fence of 1×6 pickets (actual width 5.5 in) with no gap, an 8 ft section takes 96 ÷ 5.5 = 17.5, so **18 pickets**; a 100 ft run takes about 219. Add about 5% for split and warped boards.

## Use actual width, not nominal

Lumber is named by its rough size. A "1×6" picket is about 5 1/2 in wide and a "1×4" about 3 1/2 in. Using 6 in for a 1×6 undercounts by about 8%, which on a 100 ft fence is roughly 18 pickets short. Some dog-ear pickets are sold as 5 1/2 in or 5 5/8 in; measure one.

## Gates

Leave the gate openings out of the fence length; each gate is built as its own frame, usually with the same pickets. Count gate pickets separately: a 4 ft gate with 1×6 pickets and no gap needs 48 ÷ 5.5 = 8.7, so 9.

## Worked example: an 80 ft privacy fence

80 ft of 6 ft tall fence with one 4 ft gate, 1×6 pickets, no gap, posts 8 ft apart:

1. Fence line: 80 − 4 = **76 ft** = 912 in.
2. Pickets: 912 ÷ 5.5 = 165.8 → 166, plus 5% = **175**.
3. Gate: **9 more**.
4. Posts and rails, from the same plan: 12 posts, 30 rails, 21 bags of 80 lb concrete.

The [fence calculator](/fence-calculator/) produces the whole list, including fasteners: 2 per picket at every rail, about 1,050 here.

## Shadowbox and board-on-board

A shadowbox fence alternates pickets on each side of the rails, so it looks the same from both sides and lets air through. With 1×6 pickets and a 3 1/2 in gap between pickets on each side, each side takes 96 ÷ (5.5 + 3.5) = 10.7, so 11 pickets, and an 8 ft section takes **22**, about 20% more than the 18 of a plain privacy fence. Board-on-board with a 1 in overlap works out about the same. Narrow the gaps for more privacy and the count climbs quickly.

## Tips

- **Wood shrinks.** Green pressure-treated pickets shrink as they dry, opening small gaps. Many builders butt them tight for a privacy fence.
- **Use a spacer block** cut to your gap width so the spacing stays even.
- **Check the last picket early.** Lay out a section dry; if the last picket would be a sliver, adjust the gap slightly across the section.
