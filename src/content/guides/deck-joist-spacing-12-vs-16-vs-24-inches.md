**The International Residential Code (IRC) permits 4x4 deck posts only up to a maximum height of 8 feet under light tributary loads (under 36 sq ft), while all second-story decks, heavy beam spans, and posts exceeding 8 feet in height strictly require 6x6 timber.** Furthermore, many local jurisdictions prohibit notched 4x4 posts entirely, requiring 6x6 lumber so beams can bear directly on solid wood rather than fastening hardware.

## Understanding IRC Deck Post Sizing Limits

The structural posts transfer the entire live weight (people, furniture, snow) and dead weight (framing, decking) to concrete footings. Undersized posts bow under compressive stress or fail under lateral wind loads:

- **4x4 Posts (Actual 3.5 × 3.5 in):** Approved only for ground-level or low-height platform decks up to 8 ft tall with small post tributary areas (under 36 sq ft).
- **6x6 Posts (Actual 5.5 × 5.5 in):** Required for all posts taller than 8 feet (up to 14 ft max height), multi-level decks, and any installation where the beam is notched into the post.
- **Notching Rules:** Code requires beams to bear on solid wood. A 2-ply 2x beam takes up 3.0 inches of bearing width. Notching a 4x4 leaves only 0.5 inches of wood, which violates IRC R507. A 6x6 notched for a 2-ply beam leaves a strong 2.5 inches of solid shoulder.

## Lateral Racking and Diagonal Knee Bracing

Vertical compressive strength is only one axis of structural safety. Tall decks are highly vulnerable to lateral racking (side-to-side wobble) caused by crowds walking or wind loads:

- **Diagonal Knee Bracing:** The IRC dictates that all support posts taller than 4 to 6 feet must include diagonal 2x4 or 2x6 knee braces installed at a 45-degree angle between the post and the underside of the beam or joist. Braces must be fastened using 1/2-inch through-bolts with washers, not simple deck screws.
- **Fastener Metallurgy (ACQ Corrosion):** Modern pressure-treated lumber is treated with Alkaline Copper Quaternary (ACQ) or Micronized Copper Azole (MCA), which contains high concentrations of elemental copper. Ordinary electro-galvanized or zinc-plated nails corrode through within years when exposed to copper and outdoor moisture. Use only **G185 triple-zinc hot-dipped galvanized** hardware or **Grade 304/316 stainless steel** for all post brackets, bolts, and structural structural lag screws.

## Calculating Tributary Area for Deck Posts

To verify your post size:

1. **Calculate Post Spacing:** Multiply half the beam span by half the joist span supported by the post. For a post supporting a 10 ft beam (5 ft each side) and 12 ft joists (6 ft load), the tributary area is 5 × 6 = **30 sq ft**.
2. **Select Lumber Grade:** In Southern Pine No. 2, 30 sq ft allows a 4x4 up to 8 ft high. However, upgrading to 6x6 costs less than $15 to $25 per post and provides substantially superior resistance to twisting and warping.
3. **Hardware Attachment:** Always anchor the bottom of the post to the concrete footing using elevated standoff bases (e.g. Simpson Strong-Tie ABA/ABU) to keep end-grain wood 1 inch above concrete and prevent moisture rot.
