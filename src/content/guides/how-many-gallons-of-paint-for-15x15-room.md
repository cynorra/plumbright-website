**For a standard 15x15 foot room with 8-foot ceilings, expect to need approximately 3-4 gallons of paint for two coats on both walls and ceiling, plus an additional 1-2 gallons of primer if the surface requires it.** This estimate accounts for typical door and window deductions and a 10% waste factor. However, actual quantities can vary significantly based on ceiling height, the number of doors and windows, surface porosity, and whether a color change requires multiple coats or a dedicated primer, making a detailed calculation essential.

## How the Formula Works

Accurately estimating paint quantities involves calculating the total surface area to be painted and then dividing it by the paint's coverage rate. This process accounts for both wall and ceiling surfaces, while also deducting areas that will not be painted, such as doors and windows.

The fundamental steps are:

1.  **Calculate Wall Surface Area:**
    *   Determine the room's perimeter: `(Length + Width) * 2`.
    *   Multiply the perimeter by the wall height to get the gross wall area: `Perimeter * Height`.
    *   Calculate the area of all doors and windows: `(Door Width * Door Height) + (Window Width * Window Height)`.
    *   Subtract the door and window areas from the gross wall area to get the net wall area: `Gross Wall Area - (Total Door Area + Total Window Area)`.

2.  **Calculate Ceiling Surface Area:**
    *   Multiply the room's length by its width: `Length * Width`.

3.  **Calculate Total Paintable Area:**
    *   Add the net wall area and the ceiling area: `Net Wall Area + Ceiling Area`.

4.  **Determine Paint Gallons Needed:**
    *   Most paints cover approximately 350-400 square feet per gallon for a single coat. For this guide, we will use an average of **375 sq ft/gallon**.
    *   Divide the total paintable area by the paint's coverage rate: `Total Paintable Area / Coverage Rate`.
    *   Multiply this result by the number of coats desired (typically two for optimal finish and color depth): `(Total Paintable Area / Coverage Rate) * Number of Coats`.
    *   Add a 10% waste allowance to the final gallon count to account for spills, touch-ups, and uneven absorption: `Gallons Needed * 1.10`.

5.  **Estimate Primer Gallons (if needed):**
    *   Primer coverage rates are often similar to paint, typically 300-400 sq ft per gallon. We will use **350 sq ft/gallon** for primer.
    *   Calculate primer needed for one coat: `Total Paintable Area / Primer Coverage Rate`.
    *   Add a 10% waste allowance: `Primer Gallons Needed * 1.10`.

This systematic approach ensures that all variables are considered, providing a precise estimate for your project. For a quick estimate, you can also use our [paint calculator](/paint-calculator/).

*   Room Dimensions: 15 ft (Length) x 15 ft (Width)

*   Door: 30 inches wide x 80 inches high (2.5 ft x 6.67 ft)
*   Window: 36 inches wide x 48 inches high (3 ft x 4 ft)
*   Paint Coverage: 375 sq ft/gallon
*   Primer Coverage: 350 sq ft/gallon
*   Number of Paint Coats: 2
*   Number of Primer Coats: 1
*   Waste Allowance: 10%

*   **Room Perimeter:** `(15 ft + 15 ft) * 2 = 30 ft * 2 = 60 ft`
*   **Gross Wall Area:** `60 ft * 8 ft (height) = 480 sq ft`

*   **Window Area:** `3 ft * 4 ft = 12 sq ft`
