**Divide the roof area by the roll's effective coverage (the roll's area minus the overlap at each course), add about 10% for cuts, and round up.** One roofing square is 100 sq ft. A standard 15 lb felt roll is 36 in × 144 ft, which is 432 sq ft of material but only about 408 sq ft (sold as a 4-square roll) of roof once the 2 in lap is taken out, so work from the effective figure, not the raw roll area.

## Roll area versus effective coverage

The roll area is width times length. Once courses overlap, each one covers less. For a roll that is W inches wide and laid with a head-lap of H inches, the effective coverage is:

**effective sq ft = roll area × (W − H) ÷ W**

The IRC (R905.1.1) calls for felt underlayment applied parallel to the eave and lapped 2 in horizontally, with 6 in end laps. Synthetic underlayments are lapped as the manufacturer states, often by 3 to 4 in, and low-slope roofs (below 4:12) have stricter rules, so use the lap printed on your roll. The table uses 2 in for felt and an assumed 4 in for synthetic.

Roll sizes vary by brand. The figures above are common sizes (36 in × 144 ft felt, 36 in × 72 ft for 30 lb felt, and a 48 in × 250 ft synthetic roll); recompute with the width and area on your label. End laps (6 in per joint) and cuts at hips, valleys and rakes come out of the 10% waste allowance.

## The method

1. **Roof area:** footprint × pitch multiplier. The multiplier is √(1 + (rise ÷ 12)²): 6/12 is 1.118 and 7/12 is 1.1577. The [roof pitch multiplier chart](/guides/roof-pitch-multiplier-chart/) lists the others.
2. **Squares:** roof area ÷ 100.
3. **Base rolls:** roof area ÷ effective coverage per roll.
4. **Waste:** multiply by 1.10.
5. **Round up** to whole rolls.

## Worked example: 28 × 40 ft gable roof at 7/12

1. Footprint = 28 × 40 = 1,120 sq ft.
2. Pitch multiplier for 7/12 = 1.1577, so roof area = 1,120 × 1.1577 = **1297 sq ft** (12.97 squares).
3. Rolls needed:

*   **15 lb felt:** 408 sq ft effective per roll. 1297 ÷ 408 = 3.18 rolls, × 1.10 = 3.50, so **4 rolls**.
*   **30 lb felt:** 204 sq ft effective per roll. 1297 ÷ 204 = 6.36 rolls, × 1.10 = 6.99, so **7 rolls**.
*   **Synthetic, 10-square roll:** 917 sq ft effective per roll. 1297 ÷ 917 = 1.41 rolls, × 1.10 = 1.56, so **2 rolls**.

Synthetic rolls cover far more area per roll, so you handle fewer rolls and fewer seams. Price per square, not per roll, is the fair way to compare products.
