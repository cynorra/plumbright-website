**A 12 × 12 ft room with 8 ft walls, one door and one window needs about 2 gallons of paint for two coats, so buy 2 gal.** That assumes a typical 350 sq ft per gallon coverage. The ceiling, if you paint it, takes another 1 gal of ceiling paint.

## The math for a 12 × 12 room

1. **Wall area:** perimeter × height = (12 + 12 + 12 + 12) × 8 = **384 sq ft**.
2. **Openings:** a standard door is about 21 sq ft (3 × 7 ft) and an average window about 15 sq ft. 384 − 21 − 15 = **348 sq ft** to paint.
3. **Two coats:** 348 × 2 = 696 sq ft of coverage needed.
4. **Gallons:** 696 ÷ 350 = **1.99 gallons**.

Notice how close that is to exactly 2 gallons. Leave out the door and window and it becomes 2.19 gallons, which tips the purchase to 2 gal + 1 qt. In practice, two gallons does a 12 × 12 room with normal openings, and an extra quart covers touch-ups. With 9 ft walls it's 2.26 gallons: buy 2 gal + 2 qt.

One coat is 0.99 gallons, but a single coat rarely covers well unless you're repainting the same color. Plan on two.

Read the coverage on the can you're buying, not a generic figure. Thick one-coat paints often claim *lower* square feet per gallon because they go on heavier.

## Ceilings are a separate purchase

Ceiling paint is usually flat and formulated to resist spatter, so it's a different product from wall paint. A 12 × 12 ceiling is 144 sq ft: two coats at 350 sq ft per gallon is 0.82 gallons, so one gallon is enough.

## Trim and doors

Baseboard, door casing and the door itself take very little paint by area. For one room, a quart of trim enamel is almost always enough, even for two coats.

## Tips that save a trip back

- **Buy it all at once.** Custom-tinted paint from two batches can differ slightly. If you need to mix batches, box them: pour all the cans into a bucket and stir.
- **Keep a little.** Label a small jar with the room and color for touch-ups.
- **Prime when the color changes a lot**, or over patched drywall. Primer makes the topcoat cover in fewer coats.
- **Count closets.** A reach-in closet adds about 60 to 80 sq ft of wall if you paint inside it.
